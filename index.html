High-Pressure Aerospace Burst-Test System

Eaton Aerospace Capstone | System Design & Safety

Developed a proposed burst-test system for aerospace components operating at pressures up to 50,000 psi and temperatures up to 500°F.

Four-Bar Trash-Dumper Mechanism

Machine Design | Team Project Lead

Led the development of a motor-driven four-bar crank-rocker mechanism designed to lift and dump a container within strict size, cost, and safety constraints.

Pressure Tracking and Hold Control System

Honors Controls Project | MATLAB/Simulink

Modeled and evaluated a PID pressure-control strategy for the aerospace burst-test system.

  • Created valve and pipe transfer-function models representing fast, medium, and slow dynamics.

Turbojet Performance Analysis

Honors Thermofluids Project | Python
