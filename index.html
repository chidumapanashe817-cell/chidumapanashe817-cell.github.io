About Me

From curiosity about space to a career in engineering.

My interest in engineering began when I was nine years old. During a summer visit to the library, I found a book about astronomy and astronauts and read it in just a few hours. I immediately decided I wanted to become an astronaut.

Growing up in Africa, I quickly realized that the path to becoming an astronaut was not straightforward. Instead of giving up, I began thinking about how I could still contribute to space missions. That question led me to engineering.

Today, I bring that same curiosity and determination to mechanical design, aerospace systems, manufacturing, controls, and product development. I enjoy turning technical challenges into safe, practical, and well-documented solutions.
